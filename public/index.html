Stimulation Chemistry & Fluid Selection

Practical chemistry support—fluid selection, formulation, and lab verification to ensure performance under real operating conditions (temperature, salinity, shear, logistics).

Fluid Formulation & Selection

  • System screening: slickwater, linear gels, crosslink gels, HVFR, VES/clean fluids (as applicable)
  • Fit-for-purpose selection based on rock/fluid compatibility, operational limits, and cost/performance tradeoffs
  • Additive architecture: friction reduction, hydration, crosslink/break control, surfactants, clay control, biocide, scale/corrosion strategy
  • Water quality constraints and treatment considerations (TDS, hardness, iron, sulfides, bacteria)

Testing, QA/QC & Rheology Profiling

  • Bench screening and recipe validation with clear acceptance criteria
  • Rheology profiling vs. shear rate / time / temperature (hydration, stability, break performance)
  • Compatibility checks: produced water, brines, scale tendency, emulsions, residue/cleanup indicators
  • Field QA/QC guidance: sampling plan, mixing order, on-location checks, and troubleshooting playbooks

QA/QC Focus & Field Controls

  • Mix order, hydration windows, and blender/chem unit best practices
  • On-site checks: pH, density, viscosity trend, breaker timing (as applicable)
  • Deviation handling: “if/then” actions for water changes, temperature shifts, and additive variability
  • Documentation: sampling records, acceptance criteria, and traceable batch verification
